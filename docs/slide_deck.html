Can Protein Foundation Models Beat NetMHCstabpan?

PepBuddies: High-Throughput Neoantigen Stability Prediction

Target User: Personalized cancer vaccine teams screening thousands of tumor mutation candidates across patient HLA repertoires.

The Clinical Reality: Stability > Affinity

  • 65% of immunogenic epitopes have half-life $T_{1/2} \ge 2.0\text{ h}$.
  • Stable complexes persist on the dendritic cell surface for CD8+ T-cell receptor (TCR) scanning; unstable complexes disassociate in minutes.
  • Pure binding affinity algorithms miss half of disassociating false-positive binders.

The Discovery Bottleneck

  • NetMHCstabpan-1.0 is the academic gold standard, but it is hosted on a closed web server (~2–5 s per peptide).
  • Screening a patient's 10,000 tumor somatic variants across 6 HLA alleles takes hours and cannot run inside automated pipelines.
  • The Question: Can protein language models (ESM-2, Ankh) deliver instant local inference without sacrificing accuracy?

Why Pure Foundation Models Struggle: ESM-2 was trained on folded natural proteins. Short 9-mer peptides lack secondary structure; sequence-level mean pooling washes out crucial P2/P9 anchor pocket positioning. Off-the-shelf PLMs do not beat specialized stability tools out-of-the-box.
